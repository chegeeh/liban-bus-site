About us

A coach service built on keeping its timetable

Liban Bus Ltd runs daily scheduled coaches on the Nairobi, Addis Ababa, North Eastern and Northern Kenya corridors. Our fleet is licensed, insured and GPS tracked, crews work in pairs on overnight services, and our booking desk in Eastleigh answers on the first ring.
